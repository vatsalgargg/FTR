# FTR — Product Requirements Document

Created: 2026-09-14

## Problem
Modern responsive company profile website for First Time Resolver Pvt. Ltd. using the supplied 2026 presentation. Cinematic 3D depth and smooth accessible scroll effects; verified company services, history and contact details.

## Users and jobs
- Business IT buyers assessing FTR services and contacting the team.

## Scope
- In scope: company overview, services, methodology, company milestones, partner selection, email and phone links; accessible 3D depth and scroll motion.
- Out of scope: authentication, lead collection backend, invented claims, public audience change.

## Acceptance criteria
- Navigation and contact links work; no horizontal overflow at 320px through desktop; reduced motion and keyboard navigation work; content remains readable with JavaScript disabled.
- Company facts come from supplied 14-slide 2026 profile. Source deck stays local. No analytics, secrets or visitor data collection.
